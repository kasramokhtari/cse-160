Controls
  • Hold W/S to move forward/backward and A/D to move side to side (combine for diagonals)
  • Hold Q/E to turn left/right
  • Click the world to lock the mouse (first-person look). Move the mouse to look around; press ESC to unlock the cursor.
  • X places one block, Z removes one block
Game

Press the Start Game button to begin. Your goal is to find and reach the cat tower before the zombies catch you. You have 20 seconds. If you reach it in time without getting caught, you win.

Click the Reset world button to restore the original map, or Restart Game to start a new game.
