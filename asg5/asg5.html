Controls

  • Click the world to lock the mouse for first-person look. Press ESC to release the cursor.
  • Hold W/S to move forward/backward and A/D to strafe.
  • Move the mouse to look around the world.

The Game

Press Start Game to begin. Slow zombies spawn around the map and chase you. Your goal is to navigate around the walls, pillars, and trees to reach the cat resting on the cobble shrine in the corner before a zombie catches you or your 20 seconds run out.

Press Reset World to reload the world back to its starting state, or Restart Game to play another round.

Wow Feature

Wow Feature: I added a Minecraft-style rescue game where animated zombies chase the player while the player tries to reach the cat tower before time runs out. As the timer runs down, the lighting and sky gradually shift into a sunset.
